Contents imported into this folder
---------------------------------
The 13 FBX files only. The OBJ and Blend copies shipped in the same archive are
deliberately not imported: Unity's built-in model importer reads FBX natively,
the OBJ copies carry no material assignment, and the Blend files are Blender
sources we do not open here.

Barn, BigBarn, ChickenCoop, Fence, Fence2, OpenBarn, Silo, Silo_House,
SmallBarn, TowerWindmill, WaterTower, Well, Windmill

Appearance
----------
This pack ships no textures. Each FBX carries flat-shaded solid-colour
materials (see the matching .mtl in the original archive, e.g. "DarkRed",
"RoofBlack", "White"). EmberHollow.EditorTools.ArtPackImporter converts each of
those to a shared URP/Lit material asset under Assets/Art/Materials/Quaternius/
so the buildings render correctly under URP and can be GPU instanced.
