## Goal
1. Seasonality: every shop stock entry may carry `season: [0..3]`; a stock entry
   with a season list is only available DURING those seasons. Entries without it
   remain always available. Apply the filter when the daily stock is built
   (shop:restock) and keep `extensions.shop.stock` correct.
2. Shop open days: a shop may declare `days: ['sunday', ... ]` (0-index: sunday
   first, matching core `dayOfWeek`); omitted = open every day. A shop that is
   CLOSED today must reject `shop:buy`/`shop:sell` with `shop:denied { reason:
   'closed', ... }` and hold no listed stock today.
3. Traveling merchant: new content/shops/traveling-merchant.json, `buys: false`,
   `days: ['friday']`, stock a rotating seeded selection of the six new gems plus
   a couple of rare seeds/items. Its stock must rotate deterministically per
   Friday using `rng.fork('merchant@' + dayIndex)` (see core/rng.ts + core/time.ts
   dayIndex) — same day on the same save seed -> identical stock, but different
   Fridays differ.
4. Six new items in content/items.json: `amethyst`, `emerald`, `topaz`, `opal`
   (category `mineral`), `chocolate` (category `food`), `rainbow-prism` (category
   `resource`), with sensible sell/buy prices and short descriptions (original,
   Ember Hollow flavoured — no copied names). They will be NPC gift tastes.
